## Signals → cues (deterministic, publicly fetchable)

- **Registries:** MiCA/CASP authorisations (ESMA national registers, FMA LI, FINMA,
  BaFin CSSF), Liechtenstein commercial register (new foundations/establishments,
  purpose clauses mentioning digital assets/tokenization)
- **Press/filings:** funding rounds with crypto mandate, venue memberships, license grants,
  foundation launches + treasury announcements
- **Job posts:** "MLRO", "Head of Compliance (digital assets)", "treasury manager (crypto)",
  "settlement" roles at prop/fund/exchange — hiring compliance = banking-partner gap
- **Lifecycle stage:** pre-license → licensed → scaling (pitch timing differs per stage)
